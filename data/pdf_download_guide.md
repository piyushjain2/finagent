# Annual Report PDF Download Guide — Group 11

Place all PDFs in: `data/pdfs/`
Naming: `{TICKER}_{YEAR}.pdf`  (YEAR = financial year ending, e.g. 2021 = FY2020-21)

Download all 5 years for each company: 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025
- 2021 + 2022 = forecasting INPUTS (what Claude reads to make predictions)
- 2022 + 2023 + 2024 + 2025 = ACTUALS (ground truth for accuracy comparison)
- Note: 2025 = FY2024-25 report, published ~July 2025, available as of June 2026
- Total: 20 companies × 5 years = 100 PDFs

## What to download
Priority order:
1. **TICKER_2022.pdf** — FY2021-22 (used to forecast 2023, 2024, 2025)
2. **TICKER_2021.pdf** — FY2020-21 (used to forecast 2022, 2023, 2024)
3. TICKER_2023.pdf — optional (Screener.in covers actuals)
4. TICKER_2024.pdf — optional (Screener.in covers actuals)
